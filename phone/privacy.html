TrackMyShows privacy policy

TrackMyShows is a personal app that keeps a list of TV episodes and movies you've watched, shared between your Kodi devices and your phone.

What data it uses

  • Your watch history (show, season, episode or movie, and when you watched it) is stored on your own devices and in a TrackMyShows folder in your own Google Drive.
  • The app uses the drive.file permission, so it can only see files it created itself. It cannot read anything else in your Google Drive.
  • Show titles are looked up on TMDb to get episode lists, air dates and posters.

What it does not do

  • No data is sent to the app's author or to any server other than Google Drive and TMDb.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • No data is sold or shared.

Removing your data

Delete the TrackMyShows folder from your Google Drive, and remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
